About Clara

Clara Joel is a Nigerian author who writes young adult romance, fantasy, and mystery novels, with a particular love for Christmas mystery series. Her stories are filled with romance, intriguing plotlines, unexpected twists, and memorable characters that draw readers into captivating worlds.

From heartfelt romances to fantastical adventures and mysteries that keep readers guessing, Clara enjoys creating stories that offer an escape while keeping readers turning the pages.

When she isn't writing, Clara enjoys getting lost in a good book, designing book covers, or spending time in nature.

Dive into Clara Joel's world of romance, fantasy, and mystery, and let her stories take you somewhere unforgettable, one page at a time.
