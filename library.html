YA Romance · Enemies to Lovers

You Versus Me

We were supposed to be enemies. Not us.

Zoey Hughes is barely holding her life together at Eastview High, balancing basketball, a fractured home, and pressure that never lets up. When everything starts falling apart, there’s nowhere left to hide.

Rumors spread. Friendships break. And suddenly, Zoey becomes the girl everyone turns on. False accusations, social fallout, and betrayal turn her into a target overnight.

Liam Crawford wants nothing to do with her. The school’s brooding outcast, he keeps his distance like she’s a problem he refuses to fix, but he watches her anyway.

Every clash between them is sharp and impossible to ignore, forcing proximity and tension neither of them understands. Because hate was never the only thing building between them.

Zoey learns some connections don’t ask for permission. They consume you.
